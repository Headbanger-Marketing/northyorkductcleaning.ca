Checking an unsolicited duct cleaning offer against the system in the home

An offer that arrives on its own was written before anyone looked at your North York property. You will know for certain what it includes, what it leaves out, what it asks you to decide on the spot, and which furnace or air handler and supply and return ducts you can see. Whether there is an HRV or ERV, or where the dryer vent runs, may be a guess. Mark each detail as known or guessed, so any provider who responds can quote against the system rather than the flyer.

  1. Return grilles

    Openings that pull room air back into the system.

  2. Return ducts

    The runs leading from each grille to the furnace.

  3. Furnace blower and coil

    The blower, evaporator coil and cabinet.

  4. Supply trunk

    The largest duct carrying air away from the furnace.

  5. Branch runs

    The individual runs that split off toward each room.

  6. Supply registers

    The floor, wall or ceiling vents in each room.

Separate runs to ask about:Dryer ventHRV core and screensBathroom fan ductsRange hood exhaust

Questions About Your North York Request

Is North York Duct Cleaning a cleaning company, and was it behind the offer I received?

A request sent through this North York enquiry website, owned by a marketing company, is reviewed by our enquiry team. The site is not a contractor and has no office, crew or vehicles, so it is not the business behind any flyer, call or visit you received. If a request is passed on, it goes to a provider from outside this site, and that provider has to identify itself and confirm it covers your address before anything is arranged.

How far does sending a North York request commit me?

Sending a request commits you to nothing. It does not book a visit, and it does not cancel or replace any other offer you hold. An agreement you already made with another company stays between you and that company. The form gives our enquiry team your details and a description of the offer, so that a responding provider can compare it with your system.

What should I send if I received a duct cleaning offer in North York?

Send the offer as you received it: a photo of the flyer, the text of a message or notes from a call or visit. Add the company name if one was given, what it said was included and any deadline it set. Then describe the heating equipment, ventilation and dryer vent in the home as far as you know them.

Should I agree to duct cleaning on the spot?

There is no need to decide during a call or visit. Ask for the offer in writing, including the company's full name, what is included and the total, and compare it with the system in your home. A provider who responds to a request sent through this site must also put the scope and price in writing before you agree to anything.

How can I tell whether an offer matches my home?

Compare the offer's list with what is actually installed: the number of supply registers and return grilles, the furnace or air handler, any HRV or ERV, and the dryer vent route. If the offer counts things the home does not have, or leaves out parts it does have, it was not written for your property and should be redone.

Can North York Duct Cleaning tell me if the company that called is legitimate?

No. This site cannot vouch for or investigate any company, including one that contacted you. You can ask that company for its full legal name, its address and proof of insurance, and check them yourself. Any provider introduced through this site must also identify itself and confirm its qualifications, insurance and coverage of your address directly with you.

What if someone at the door says my ducts or furnace are unsafe?

Ask them to write down what they saw, where they saw it and why they think it is unsafe. A claim made at the door should not decide the work. Questions about gas, combustion, carbon monoxide or electrical safety belong with an appropriately qualified technician or, where there is immediate danger, emergency responders. Keep any written note with your request.

Is it reasonable to get a second quote after an unsolicited North York offer?

Yes. An offer you did not ask for is one starting point, and comparing it with a quote built from an inspection of your own system is a normal step. Give both parties the same description of the equipment, ducts and vents, so the two written scopes can be compared line by line rather than by the headline total alone.
